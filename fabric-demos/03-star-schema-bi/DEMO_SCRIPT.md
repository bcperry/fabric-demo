# Demo 03 Script

1. Open **Site Readiness & Recovery** on the mirrored lead event. Read the actual
	event window, visible scope, unresolved finding, accountable organization,
	next action, and due date.
2. Select a site and verify its casework narrows; reset and verify totals return.
	A finding selection may narrow casework further without changing site context.
3. Explain that normal/non-normal counts describe synthetic participant profiles,
	not certified readiness. Neither an empty view nor zero findings is approval.
4. Open an attached evidence reference. Establish its identity and availability;
	do not call it latest, complete, or accepted closure evidence without proof.
5. Use **Readiness Detail** for the separate analytical readiness scope. Do not
	imply that its filters or totals govern the mirrored casework page.
6. For a rehearsed administrative update, observe mirroring, rebuild the gold
	products, refresh the semantic model, and verify the same action's details.
	Mirroring alone does not refresh derived gold tables.
7. Show source lineage and Silver rejection evidence only after the audience
	understands the review question. Geography is public; operational associations
	and data are fictional.

Acceptance: no automatic authorization claim; coherent action/owner/deadline;
site-scoped casework; explicit record timestamp and remaining review limitations.

See the [audience walkthrough](../../DEMO_WALKTHROUGH.md) for the opening issue,
cross-fixture boundary, and unavailable-service fallback.

Expected time: 20 minutes.
